# OwnNotes — Your Offline Private AI Notebook

Turn your iPhone and iPad into a private AI-powered notebook that helps you capture, organize, summarize, and improve your ideas—completely offline.

**No cloud. No account. No subscription.**

Everything runs directly on your device, so your notes stay private while you enjoy fast, intelligent AI assistance anywhere, even without an internet connection. Whether you're taking meeting notes, studying, journaling, brainstorming, writing, or organizing research, your AI assistant is always available.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **100% Offline AI:** All AI processing happens on your device. No cloud processing or internet required.
* **Live Voice-to-Text:** Capture ideas naturally with fast and accurate speech transcription.
* **AI Summaries:** Turn long notes into concise summaries and actionable takeaways.
* **Rewrite with AI:** Improve grammar, clarity, tone, and structure with a single tap.
* **Simplify Complex Info:** Convert difficult topics into easy-to-understand explanations.
* **Brainstorm Ideas:** Generate outlines, titles, creative ideas, and writing suggestions from your notes.
* **Biometric Lock:** Protect your notes with Face ID or Touch ID for an extra layer of privacy.
* **Easy Sharing:** Instantly share your notes and AI-generated content with Mail, Apple Notes, Reminders, Messages, and your favorite apps.
* **Private by Design:** Your notes never leave your device.
* **Works Anywhere:** Perfect for flights, travel, classrooms, meetings, or anywhere without internet access.
